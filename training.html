Certification Pathways

Two tracks. One goal: job-ready skills.

Our two flagship pathways — Certified Ethical Hacker preparation and CCNA networking foundations — go deeper than the overview above, with a structured curriculum built around what employers actually test for.

Ethical Hacking

CEH — Certified Ethical Hacker Preparation

A structured preparation track for the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) exam, built around the five phases of ethical hacking: reconnaissance, scanning, gaining access, maintaining access, and covering tracks — taught with hands-on labs rather than slides alone.

Curriculum covers

  • Footprinting & reconnaissance
  • Scanning networks & enumeration
  • Vulnerability analysis
  • System hacking techniques
  • Malware threats & analysis
  • Sniffing & social engineering
  • Web application & API attacks
  • Wireless & mobile security
  • Cloud & IoT security basics
  • Cryptography fundamentals

Networking

CCNA — Cisco Networking Fundamentals

A ground-up networking course aligned to CCNA exam topics — the foundation nearly every other security specialty builds on. Strong network fundamentals make every later course (pentesting, cloud, network security) click faster.

Curriculum covers

  • Network fundamentals & the OSI model
  • IP addressing & subnetting
  • Routing concepts & protocols
  • Switching, VLANs & trunking
  • Wireless networking basics
  • Network access control & ACLs
  • IP services (NAT, DHCP, NTP)
  • Network security fundamentals
  • Automation & programmability basics
