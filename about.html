CLEAR REPORTING FOR EVERY AUDIENCE

Built for the way real organizations make decisions

Security findings only matter if the right people can act on them. That's why every engagement produces two layers of reporting: a technical breakdown engineers can act on immediately, and an executive summary leadership can use to prioritize budget and risk — without needing a security background to follow it. We work with startups, growing SaaS companies, universities, and organizations that need an outside, adversarial perspective on their own systems.
